## What it does

Your design system, ready to drop into any template.

- **Save any selection** — Hero, footer, product grid, unsubscribe block. Pick the blocks on the canvas, name them, and they land in the library.
- **Browse with a live preview** — A searchable library renders every entry as it will look, so you pick the right one before inserting.
- **Insert where you want it** — At the top, after any existing block, or at the end.
- **Workspace-wide** — Everyone on your team draws from the same library, stored in Cloud with nothing for you to host.

## Good fits

- Agencies shipping dozens of templates with a consistent footer
- Product teams with a branded hero used in every transactional email
- Anyone tired of rebuilding the same section from scratch
